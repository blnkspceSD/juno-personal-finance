# Mission: Juno Personal Finance

## Pitch
Juno is a personal finance tool that implements YNAB's proven envelope budgeting method to help working adults gain complete control over their finances through intentional spending allocation and real-time expense tracking.

## Users

### Primary User Persona: Working Adults (Ages 20-40)
- **Income Range**: $35,000 - $150,000 annually
- **Financial Stage**: Early career to established professionals building financial stability
- **Pain Points**: Living paycheck to paycheck despite decent income, inability to save consistently, unclear spending patterns, financial stress
- **Goals**: Build emergency fund, eliminate debt, save for major purchases, achieve financial peace of mind
- **Technology Comfort**: High smartphone usage, comfortable with web apps, values simplicity over complexity

## The Problem

### 1. Lack of Intentional Spending Control
73% of working adults don't know where their money goes each month, leading to overspending in discretionary categories while underfunding important goals like emergency savings and debt repayment.

### 2. Traditional Budgeting Tools Are Reactive
Most budgeting apps focus on tracking past spending rather than proactive allocation, leaving users to guess whether they can afford purchases and making it difficult to prioritize financial goals.

### 3. Financial Stress from Uncertainty
Without clear visibility into available funds for each spending category, users experience constant anxiety about whether they can afford purchases, leading to either restrictive spending that feels unsustainable or impulsive spending that derails financial goals.

### 4. Complex Tools Overwhelm Simple Needs
Existing financial tools are either too basic (simple expense trackers) or overwhelmingly complex (enterprise-level budgeting software), failing to serve working adults who need structured guidance without complexity.

## Differentiators

### 1. Envelope Method Implementation
Unlike traditional budgeting apps that track spending after the fact, Juno implements true envelope budgeting where every dollar is allocated before spending, providing clear spending limits and preventing overspending.

### 2. Real-Time Decision Support
Instant visibility into available funds in each category at the point of purchase, eliminating guesswork and providing confidence in spending decisions through mobile-optimized interfaces.

### 3. Simplicity-First Design
Focused specifically on the core envelope budgeting workflow without overwhelming users with investment tracking, credit score monitoring, or other tangential features that complicate the primary use case.

## Key Features

### Core Budgeting Features
1. **Monthly Budget Allocation**: Simple interface to assign monthly income to spending categories (envelopes)
2. **Envelope Balance Tracking**: Real-time view of remaining funds in each budget category
3. **Budget Reallocation**: Easy transfer of funds between categories when priorities change
4. **Overspending Alerts**: Immediate notifications when approaching or exceeding category limits

### Transaction Management
5. **Quick Expense Entry**: One-tap expense logging with category assignment and photo receipt capture
6. **Transaction History**: Searchable history of all expenses with filtering by category, date, and amount
7. **Recurring Transaction Setup**: Automatic handling of regular bills and income with customizable schedules
8. **Bank Import Integration**: Optional automated transaction import from major banks and credit cards

### Dashboard & Analytics
9. **Monthly Overview Dashboard**: Clear visualization of budget vs. actual spending across all categories with progress indicators
10. **Spending Trends**: Simple charts showing spending patterns over time to identify opportunities for improvement

### User Experience Features
11. **Mobile-First Design**: Optimized for quick expense entry and balance checking on smartphones
12. **Offline Capability**: Core functionality works without internet connection with sync when reconnected
