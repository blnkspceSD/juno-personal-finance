# Mission Lite: Juno Personal Finance

## Elevator Pitch
Juno implements YNAB's envelope budgeting method to help working adults gain complete control over their finances through intentional spending allocation.

## Core Value
Juno serves working adults earning $35-150k annually by providing real-time envelope budgeting that eliminates financial uncertainty. Unlike reactive expense trackers, Juno requires users to allocate every dollar before spending, preventing overspending while maintaining simplicity over complex financial management tools.
